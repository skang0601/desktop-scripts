# Delegation

When delegation is appropriate and permitted by higher-priority instructions, use the local-model explore and general subagents for bounded, straightforward tasks. Use the local-model coder subagent for substantial, self-contained implementation and debugging work where a coding specialist is useful. Give it the full task and relevant context in one delegation, rather than alternating between local models during a task: loading Devstral takes about 10 seconds, while returning to Qwen takes about 40 seconds. Prefer Qwen for exploration, general work, multimodal tasks, and decisions that need its 64K context or controllable reasoning. Keep complex decisions and integration with the primary agent, and verify subagent findings and changes before relying on them.
